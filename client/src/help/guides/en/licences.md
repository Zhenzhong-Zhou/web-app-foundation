# Licences

Licences are the numbers your formulations are registered under, such as a natural health product’s NPN in Canada. Recipes are made under them, so a batch traces back to its licence. You reach this page from **Licences** on Products.

## Add a licence {#add}

1. Press **Add licence**.
2. Type the number as issued, and who issued it, such as Health Canada.
3. Give the date it was issued, if you have it.
4. Leave **Valid until** blank for a scheme that does not expire. An NPN does not.
5. Press **Save**.

Then choose it on a recipe, under **Licence**.

## Keep it current {#current}

1. Press **Edit** on the licence.
2. Change **Valid until** when it is renewed, or turn off **Current** when it is withdrawn.
3. Press **Save**.

Withdrawn and expired licences stay listed, so a batch made under one still traces back. New recipes stop offering a withdrawn one.

Whoever may change licences or release production runs is told in the bell 60, 30 and 7 days before a licence expires, and on the day. What a run does with an expired licence is your organization’s choice: see its [licence policy](/settings/organization#licences).

> Only someone who may add licences sees **Add licence**, and only someone who may change them sees **Edit**.

## Words you’ll meet {#words}

- **Current**: in force today.
- **In force from**: issued from a later day, and usable from then.
- **Expires in**: within 60 days of its **Valid until** day.
- **Expired**: past its **Valid until** day.
- **Withdrawn**: no longer used, kept for what was made under it.

## If something is refused {#refused}

- A number already recorded for the same issuer is refused. Edit the one already listed.
- A licence cannot expire before it was issued. Check the two dates.
