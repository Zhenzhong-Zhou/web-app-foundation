# Audit log

The audit log lists every change made in this organization, newest first: what was done, to which record, by whom, when and from where. Entries are kept for two years, and nobody can edit or remove them.

## Find a change {#find}

1. Choose an **Action**, or keep **All actions**.
2. Set **From** and **To** to narrow it to some days.
3. Press **Load more** for older entries.

Where an entry shows a field with an arrow, it changed from the first value to the second; a value on its own is what the field was set to. **Export** downloads the entries your filters show, as a CSV file.

## One record, or one person {#record}

- **History** on a record’s page shows its recent changes beside it, and opens the rest here.
- A person’s page on [People](/people) opens all of their activity here.

The log then says it is showing one record, or one person, only. **Show all** goes back to everything.

> Only someone who may read the audit log sees this page, and **History** on records.

## Your own activity {#yours}

Everyone can see what they did themselves: **See all your work** on [Your account](/account). Your sign-ins and password changes are on **Your devices**.

## Words you’ll meet {#words}

- **By**: the member who made the change.
- **A removed account**: someone whose account was deleted. What they did stays in the log.

## If something is refused {#refused}

- Without access, the page says your role does not include the audit log. Ask someone who may change roles.

See also [People](/people).
