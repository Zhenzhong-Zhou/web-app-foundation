# Audit log

The audit log lists every change made in this organization, newest first: what was done, to which record, by whom, when and from where. Entries are kept for two years, and nobody can edit or remove them.

## Find a change {#find}

1. Choose an **Action**, or keep **All actions**.
2. Set **From** and **To** to narrow it to some days.
3. Press **Load more** for older entries.

Where an entry shows a field with an arrow, it changed from the first value to the second; a value on its own is what the field was set to. An entry with two changes or more shows each on its own line, the field and its old value → its new one, after a first line that holds the details that are not changes, such as the record. From seven changes it shows the first five, with **Show all 7 changes** to open the rest in place and **Show fewer** to close them again. Nothing is cut, and a record’s **History** reads the same. **Export** downloads the entries your filters show, as a CSV file.

## One record, or one person {#record}

- **History** on a record’s page shows its recent changes beside it, and opens the rest here.
- A person’s page on [People](/people) opens all of their activity here.

The log then says it is showing one record, or one person, only. **Show all** goes back to everything.

> Only someone who may read the audit log sees this page, and **History** on records.

## A lot’s entry {#lot}

“Lot updated” names the lot and its item, and keeps each field the save changed, before → after: the lot code, the supplier’s lot, the expiry’s kind and its day. A corrected code keeps both, “Lot code: ASH-06O1 → ASH-0601”. Why the code changed is not written here: it is kept with the old code under **Former codes** on the lot’s page, which stays as long as the lot does, where the log keeps its entries two years. See [Trace a lot](/lots#code).

## Your own activity {#yours}

Everyone can see what they did themselves: **See all your work** on [Your account](/account). Your sign-ins and password changes are on **Your devices**.

## Words you’ll meet {#words}

- **By**: the member who made the change.
- **A removed account**: someone whose account was deleted. What they did stays in the log.

## If something is refused {#refused}

- Without access, the page says your roles don’t include the audit log. Ask someone who may change roles.

See also [People](/people).
