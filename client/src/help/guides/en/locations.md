# Locations

Locations are where stock is kept, from a whole site down to one bin. They form a tree, and stock sits only in a location with nothing inside it.

## Add a location {#add}

1. Press **Add location** for one at the top, such as a site, or **Add inside** on a location for one within it.
2. Choose its type: a site, a zone, an aisle, a shelf or a bin. It is a label for reading, not a rule.
3. Give its name, and its code as on the label, such as A-01-03. A code is used only once within the same location.
4. Press **Add location**.

A single room can be a location in its own right: add bins inside it later if you need them.

## Give a site its address {#address}

Press **Address** on a site to say where it is. Purchase orders ask for delivery there, and returns are sent back there. Without one, they print the organization’s registered address. Places inside a site share its address.

## Change or retire a location {#edit}

1. Press **Edit** on the location.
2. Change its name, its code, or the location it sits **Inside**.
3. Turn off **Available for picking** for stock that must not be sent, such as returns waiting to be checked.
4. Turn off **In use** to retire it. A location is retired rather than deleted, so its history stays.
5. Press **Save**.

> Only someone who may add locations sees **Add location**, and only someone who may change them sees **Add inside**, **Address** and **Edit**.

## Words you’ll meet {#words}

- **Site**, **Zone**, **Aisle**, **Shelf** and **Bin**: a location’s type, outermost first.
- **Holds stock**: a location with nothing inside it, where stock can sit.
- **Not available**: its stock is not for sending.
- **Retired**: no longer in use, kept for its history.

## If something is refused {#refused}

- A location that holds stock cannot have one added inside it. Move its stock to another location first.
- A code already used within the same location is refused. Choose another.
- A location cannot be moved inside itself or anything within it.
