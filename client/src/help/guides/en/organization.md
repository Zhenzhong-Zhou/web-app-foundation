# Organization

How the organization works here: its profile, money, documents, stock, branding and security, each a tab. Anyone can read them.

> Only someone who may change the organization’s settings sees the save buttons, and the **Branding** and **Security** tabs.

## Profile {#profile}

On **Profile**, every invoice prints the name, tax number and address as the seller, as they stood on the day it was issued.

1. Fill in the **Organization name**, the **Tax registration number** and the **Registered address**, saving each.
2. Under **What we do**, write one line about what the organization does, in your own words, and press **Save what we do**. Home shows it to every member, under the date.
3. Choose the **Time zone** where the business is, and press **Save time zone**. It decides the organization’s day, such as when a licence expires.

Downloading everything and closing the organization are at its end.

## Money {#money}

On **Money**, set the **Base currency**, what stock is valued in, before stock arrives: anything received earlier waits for a cost. The **Default sale price list** serves customers with no list of their own.

## Documents {#documents}

On **Documents**, say which language items are named in, and what documents print in. Each section has its own save button, off until something changes.

1. Under **Item names**, choose **Items are named in**: the language each item’s own name, the one it was added with, is typed in. A new organization starts with the language of the person who started it; one from before reads **Not set** until it is chosen, and once chosen it can be changed but not cleared.
2. Under **Names documents require**, tick each language in which every item on a document must have a name: a document printing in it cannot be issued while one has none. The language items are named in is ticked already, and reads **Met by each item’s own name**. While a language is ticked and none is chosen, a line says to choose one.
3. Press **Save item names**. Choosing a language asks first.
4. Under **Document languages**, choose what packing slips, invoices and credit notes print in, **Documents print in** and **Then also in** for a bilingual sheet, and press **Save document languages**. Documents already made keep theirs.
5. Under **Logo on documents**, say whether invoices, credit notes and packing slips print the logo, and press **Save logo on documents**. Until there is a logo, the section says to add one on **Branding**.

Choosing a language asks first. The confirmation says how many own names will count as that language’s names, and that nothing is translated. A name already kept in that language that is the same as the own one is removed as a copy. A name that differs is listed beside the own one: choose which to keep on each row, or press a column’s heading to choose it on every row shown, and **Only those left** shows the rows still to choose. The button that begins **Name items in** saves the choice, and the message says how many copies were removed and items renamed. This cannot be undone: the language can be changed, never cleared, and the names removed cannot be brought back.

## Numbering {#numbering}

On **Documents**, each kind of document has its own series. A change applies from the next document; a number already given never changes.

1. Type the **Prefix**, or start from a common format. Add the date with its buttons, as **{YYYY}**, **{YY}**, **{MM}** or **{DD}**: with the year, invoices print as INV-2026-0455.
2. Choose the **Digits**, and when it **Starts again**: never, each year, each month or each day.
3. Set the **Next number**, press **Save numbering**, then **Change numbering**.

The same section numbers **Lot codes**: the codes made for a new lot when it is received, L-2026-11-21-1 by default, starting again at 1 each day. Their prefix holds only capital letters, digits, - and the date, and under the next code the row says how many codes fit within 20 characters, what a barcode’s lot field holds.

## Licences at release {#licences}

On **Stock**, choose what releasing a run does when the licence on its recipe is not yet in force, or has expired. A withdrawn licence is always refused.

1. For **Not yet in force** and for **Expired**, choose **Refuse**, **Refuse unless overridden, with a reason**, or **Allow, and record it**.
2. Tick **A recipe must carry a licence to be released** if every run needs one, and press **Save licence policy**.

## Branding {#branding}

On **Branding**, choose how the app looks for everyone here.

1. Under **Logo**, press **Upload a logo**. Wide or square, it heads the sidebar, and prints on invoices, credit notes and packing slips unless **Documents** says otherwise.
2. Under **Square logo**, press **Upload a square logo** for small spaces, such as the folded sidebar. The line under it says what shows there without one.
3. Choose a **Colour** and the **Sidebar**’s shade, and press **Save branding**.

## Sign out after inactivity {#idle-sign-out}

On **Security**, for computers several people share.

1. Under **After**, choose how long without use, from 15 minutes to 4 hours, or **Off**.
2. Under **Applies to**, choose only sign-ins without **Keep me signed in**, or everyone, and press **Save**.

A minute before, people see a warning with **Stay signed in**. It can only shorten a session, never lengthen it.

## Words you’ll meet {#words}

- **Registered address**: the seller’s address every invoice prints.
- **Override**: a run released despite its licence, its reason kept.

## If something is refused {#refused}

- No invoice is issued until the registered address is set.
- The base currency cannot change once stock carries a value in it.
- Choosing the language items are named in saves nothing while a row to choose has no choice: the row says **Choose one to keep**.
- A name that changed while the confirmation was open saves nothing: it says so and shows the list again, a row gone named, a new one marked, the other choices kept.
- At most 500 names that differ can be chosen at once. Change some first with **Edit names** on their items, then choose again.
- A next number below one already given is refused; the lowest allowed is offered.
- Two kinds of document with one prefix would print the same numbers: give each its own.
- A square logo must be about square, at most 5 : 4 either way, and at least 128 pixels on each side. Use a wide image as the main logo.
