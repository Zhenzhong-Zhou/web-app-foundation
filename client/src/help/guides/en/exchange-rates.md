# Exchange rates

What one unit of another currency is worth in your base currency, day by day. A purchase priced in another currency is valued at the latest rate on or before the day it arrived.

## Set a rate {#add}

1. Press **Set a rate**.
2. Choose the **Currency** and the **Day**.
3. Type the **Rate**: how many units of the base currency one unit of that currency is worth that day.
4. Press **Save rate**.

A purchase that arrives with no rate on file for its currency waits for a cost on [Stock value](/costs#costs) until one is given.

## Correct a rate {#correct}

1. On the rate’s row, press **Correct**.
2. Type the right **Rate**, and press **Save rate**.

Correcting a rate changes nothing already valued: it applies to what is valued from then on.

> Only someone who may change costs sees **Set a rate** and **Correct**.

## Words you’ll meet {#words}

- **Base currency**: what stock is valued in, set on the organization’s **Money** tab.
- **Rate**: how much of the base currency one unit of another is worth, on a day.

## If something is refused {#refused}

- No base currency is set yet. Set one on [Organization](/settings/organization) first, since a rate converts into it.
- The base currency itself takes no rate. A rate converts other currencies into it.
- A rate must be greater than zero.

See also [Stock value](/costs).
