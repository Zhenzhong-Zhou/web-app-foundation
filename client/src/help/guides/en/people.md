# People

People lists everyone in your organization, with their photo or initials, job title and work phone, so you know whom to ask and how to reach them. Everyone who works here can see it.

## Find someone {#find}

1. Search by name, job title, department or email.
2. Open the person to see their department, location, work phone and extension, email, and since when they have been a member.

Each person shows roughly when they were last active. Whoever joined in the last two weeks is marked **New**, with the day they joined, so you know whom to greet, and the page says how many joined.

## What someone has done {#activity}

A person’s page also lists the last 20 things they did here, with a link to all of their activity in the audit log.

> Only someone who may read the audit log sees a person’s recent activity.

## Your own details {#your-details}

What colleagues see of you is yours to set, on [Your account](/account): a photo, and your job title, department, location, work phone and extension. Nobody else can change them.

## Words you’ll meet {#words}

- **Role**: what the person may do here, such as Owner, Admin or Viewer.
- **Active today**, **this week** or **this month**: roughly when they last used the app.
- **Never signed in**: a member who has not signed in yet.
- **New**: joined in the last two weeks.

## If something is refused {#refused}

- A page that says the person isn’t in your organization belongs to someone who has left, or was never a member. Their past work keeps their name.

See also [Members](/members).
