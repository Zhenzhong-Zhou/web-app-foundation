# Your account

Your account holds what is yours rather than the organization’s: your name, photo and language, your details at work, your password and devices, and what you have done here. Only you can change it.

## Your access {#your-access}

Your access at the organization shows your roles, each with a line saying what it is for, and what you can do here with them together, area by area. It ends with whom to ask for more: up to three people who may change roles, each name opening their page on [People](/people). Someone who may do everything reads that in one line.

## Say who you are {#profile}

1. Press **Add a photo**: PNG, JPEG or WebP, up to 5 MB. Without one, colleagues see your initials.
2. Under **Profile**, change your name or language and press **Save**. Emails reach you in the language you choose.
3. Under your details at the organization, add your job title, department, location, work phone and extension, and press **Save details**. Each is optional, and colleagues see them on People.

## Sign-in and security {#security}

1. Press **Change password**, give your current password, then the new one twice.
2. Press **Your devices** to see every device signed in as you. **Sign out** any you do not recognise, and change your password.

Your sign-ins and password changes of the last ninety days are listed on **Your devices** too. Everything you did in this organization, newest first, is under **See all your work**.

## Leave or delete {#leave}

1. Press **Download your data** first if you want a copy of what is yours.
2. **Leave** the organization: you lose access at once, your account stays, and your work there keeps your name.
3. **Delete my account** asks for your password, and deletes it after 30 days. Until then, keep it from the banner on every page. If you are the organization’s only member, you are asked to delete both together.

## Words you’ll meet {#words}

- **Role**: what you may do in this organization, such as Owner, Admin or Viewer.
- **This device**: the one you are using now. Sign it out with **Sign out** in your account menu.

## If something is refused {#refused}

- The only Owner cannot leave while others are members. Make another member an Owner on [Members](/members) first.
- The only member cannot leave. Invite someone and make them an Owner, or close the organization.
- A wrong password is refused. Check it and try again, or reset it from the sign-in page.
- Your email address cannot be changed yet.
