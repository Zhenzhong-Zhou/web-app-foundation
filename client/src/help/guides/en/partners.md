# Partners

A partner is a firm you buy from, sell to, or both: one list for both sides of the trade. Whether a partner is a supplier or a customer follows from the orders you raise with them, so there is nothing to choose when you add one.

## Add a partner {#add}

1. Press **Add partner**.
2. Type the name. Names need not be unique: two branches of one company are two partners.
3. Add your own **Code** for them and their **Tax ID**, if you use them.
4. Press **Add partner**.

> Only someone who may add partners sees **Add partner**.

## Addresses and contacts {#addresses}

1. Open the partner.
2. Press **Add address**. Give it a label you would say out loud, such as Head office, and say whether **Invoices go here**, **Deliveries go here**, and whether to **Use this one by default**.
3. Press **Add contact** for whoever answers when an order is late, with an email or a phone. **Main contact** marks the first one to call.

An invoice is issued only to a customer with a billing address, so add one before their first invoice.

## Prices and document languages {#settings}

On the partner’s page, **Sales to them** and **Purchases from them** choose the price list that prices a line added without a price. **Document languages** chooses what their packing slips, invoices and credit notes print in, from the next one made.

> Only someone who may change partners sees **Edit**, **Add address**, **Add contact** and **Retire**, and can save the price lists and languages.

## Retire a partner {#retire}

Nothing here is deleted, since orders name it. To stop using a partner, press **Edit** and turn off **In use**: they stay readable wherever they appear, and leave the list an order is raised from. An address or a contact is retired with **Retire**.

## Words you’ll meet {#words}

- **Code**: your own reference for the partner, unique in your organization.
- **Default**: the address chosen first when more than one would do.
- **Retired**: no longer used, kept for the records that name it.

## If something is refused {#refused}

- A code another partner already uses is refused. Choose another, or leave it blank.
- The default address cannot be retired. Make another address the default first.
