# Movements

Movements are every change to a quantity, newest first: receipts, shipments, transfers, corrections, production and returns. They are never edited or removed; a mistake is corrected by another movement.

## Find a movement {#find}

1. Choose a period under **Recorded**, such as **This month**.
2. Narrow it to one location, or to one reason under **Why**.
3. Press **Load more** to see older rows.

Each row says when, the item and its lot, the change, where, why and by whom.

## Export the list {#export}

Press **Export** to download the movements your filters show as a CSV file, for a spreadsheet.

## Correct a mistake {#correct}

A movement cannot be changed. To put a quantity right, use **Correct the count** or **Move** on [Inventory](/inventory). Each adds a movement of its own, so the history says what happened and who did it.

## Words you’ll meet {#words}

- **receipt**: stock arriving, on an order or without one.
- **shipment**: stock leaving, for a customer or with no order.
- **transfer**: stock moved between locations.
- **adjustment**: a corrected count.
- **production** and **consumption**: what a run made, and what it used.
- **sample**: stock given away.
- **return**: stock a customer sent back.
- **Deleted user**: someone whose account was deleted. Their movements stay.

See also [Inventory](/inventory) and [Lots](/lots).
