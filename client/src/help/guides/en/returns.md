# Returns

A return authorization, or RMA, says what a customer may send back from a sale, and whether it is credited, replaced or neither. You raise one from the sale it concerns.

## Authorize a return {#authorize}

1. Open the sale on [Orders](/orders) and press **Authorize a return**.
2. Say **Why is it coming back**, in the customer’s words.
3. For each item, enter how much may come back, and **Then**: **Credit**, **Replace** or **No credit or replacement**.
4. Leave **The customer is sending the goods back** ticked if a box will arrive, and choose where it comes back to. Untick it if they were told to keep or destroy the goods: credit then follows what you authorize.
5. Press **Authorize**. Its PDF can be sent to the customer.

> Only someone who may authorize returns sees **Authorize a return**, and only someone who may change them sees **Close RMA**, **Cancel RMA**, **Link a return** and **Raise replacement**.

## Receive the goods {#receive}

1. When the box arrives, open the sale and press **Take a return**.
2. Choose the RMA under **Against RMA**, and where to **Put it in**: usually a bin marked not available, so nothing ships it before it is checked.
3. Press **Receive return**. Nothing is restocked yet: once checked, move it to a shelf, or correct the count if it is written off.

Goods that arrived before the RMA was raised are counted against it with **Link a return**, once.

> Only someone who may receive goods sees **Take a return**.

## Credit or replace {#resolve}

- For a credit, press **Credit on** and the invoice’s number on the RMA, then issue the credit note there.
- For a replacement, press **Raise replacement**: a draft sale opens with the items to replace, at zero. Confirm and ship it as any sale.

> Only someone who may issue invoices sees **Credit on**.

When nothing more will happen, press **Close RMA**. **Cancel RMA** is offered only while nothing has come back or been credited.

## Words you’ll meet {#words}

- **Open**: more may still come back or be credited.
- **Closed**: finished. What was received and credited stays.
- **Cancelled**: it never happened.

## If something is refused {#refused}

- No more can be authorized than the customer holds, shipped less returned.
- A sample was never billed, so it cannot be credited. Replace it, or take it back with no credit or replacement.
- A replacement for a retired customer cannot be raised. Put them back in use on [Partners](/partners) first.
- An RMA that has a credit against it is closed rather than cancelled.

See also [Invoices](/invoices).
