# Roles

Roles lists what each role lets its holders do here, and who holds it. Everyone can read it; only Owners make, change and delete roles.

## What a role is {#what}

A role is a set of permissions, each one thing it allows, such as **Receive stock against an order** or **See prices on orders**. A role’s page says what it can do, in the words its holders read on [Your account](/account#your-access), what they are also told about, and who holds it.

Owner, Admin and Viewer are built in. Beside them, an organization makes roles for the work its people do: one for the warehouse, one for the books.

## Make a role {#make}

1. Press **Make a role**.
2. Under **Start from**, choose a template, such as **Warehouse** or **Sales**, or **A copy of a role** you have.
3. Give it a name, and a line saying what it is for if you like.
4. Tick what it allows, area by area. **Tick all** and **Clear** do a whole area at once.
5. Press **Save role**.

A template only ticks boxes, and you can change any of them. Ticking a permission ticks what it needs with it, such as seeing orders for receiving against them, and unticking one unticks whatever needs it; a line above the areas says what changed with it. What the role can do stands beside the boxes, or below them on a phone, and follows each tick.

> Every role sees who works here, the roles and the organization’s settings, so those three stay ticked. Making and changing roles, and closing the organization, stay with Owners and are never part of a role.

## Several roles {#several}

A member can hold several roles, and may do whatever any of them allows. Someone who works in the warehouse and takes sales calls holds **Warehouse** and **Sales**, rather than a role made for the pair. Take one away, and they keep what the other allows. Change a role, and everyone who holds it has the change on their next click.

An Owner holds no other role, since Owner already includes everything. Roles are given on [Members](/members#role), and when you [invite someone](/members#invite).

## You give only what you hold {#give}

You can give a role only when your own roles, together, include everything it can do. Changing or removing a member asks the same of their roles. So nobody can give more than they have, and only an Owner can make someone an Owner.

On Members and in Invite, a role you cannot give is greyed, with the reason.

## Why the built-in roles stay as they are {#built-in}

Owner, Admin and Viewer cannot be renamed, changed or deleted. Their names and lines are the app’s own, in every language, and a new permission reaches them in every organization at once; an edited one would go on saying what it no longer does. To vary one, open it and press **Make a copy**: the copy starts with everything it has, and you untick what you don’t want. **Move everyone to…** then moves its holders to the copy in one step.

## Name roles in your team’s language {#naming}

A role you make reads as you named it, in every language: a role named 仓库 reads 仓库 to someone using the app in English. So name roles in the language your team reads. No two roles share a name, whatever their capitals, and none may read as Owner, Admin or Viewer in any of the app’s languages.

## Change, empty or delete a role {#change}

1. Open the role and press **Edit** to change its name, its line or what it allows. Everyone who holds it has the change on their next click.
2. Press **Move everyone to…** to move every member who holds it to another role, which they hold instead.
3. Press **Delete** to remove a role that nobody holds and no waiting invitation names.

> Only Owners see **Edit** and **Delete**, and only on a role made here. **Move everyone to…** is for whoever may change members’ roles, and offers only the roles they could give.

## Words you’ll meet {#words}

- **Permission**: one thing a role allows, such as **See prices on orders**.
- **Built in**: Owner, Admin and Viewer, the same in every organization.
- **Made here**: a role this organization made, from a template or a copy.
- **Template**: a start for a new role, such as Warehouse or Sales. It only ticks boxes.
- **Also told**: what a role’s holders are notified of, such as an order line closed short.

## If something is refused {#refused}

- A name another role has, whatever its capitals, or one that reads as a built-in role, is refused. Choose another.
- A permission saved without one it needs is refused, naming what it needs. Tick that too.
- A role someone else saved while you were editing it is not overwritten. Press **Reload** to see their changes, then make yours again.
- A role someone holds is not deleted: move them to another role first. Nor is one that an invitation waiting to be accepted names: revoke the invitation, or wait until it is accepted.
- A role that can do more than your own roles is not given. An Owner can give it.
