# Members

Members lists who belongs to the organization and the roles each holds, and is where someone new is invited. Everyone signs in with their own email and password: nobody sets a password for anyone else.

## Invite someone {#invite}

1. Press **Invite**.
2. Type their email address, and tick their roles.
3. Choose the language to write to them in, and add a note if you like. It shows in the email, and on the page where they accept.
4. Press **Send invitation**, or **Copy link** to send the link yourself, to them alone.

The link works once, for 7 days, and nobody is a member until they accept. Until then the invitation waits under **Invited**, where you can **Resend** it, **Copy link** again or **Revoke** it.

Once the organization has made a role of its own, no role is ticked to start with, so nobody joins as a Viewer unless you choose it.

> Only someone who may invite sees **Invite**, and only an Owner can invite an Owner. A role you cannot give is greyed, with the reason.

## Change roles {#role}

A member can hold several roles, and may do whatever any of them allows. [Roles](/settings/roles) says what each one can do.

1. On the member’s row, open its actions and choose **Change roles**.
2. Tick the roles they should hold. Beneath them, what they could do with the roles ticked follows each tick.
3. Press **Save roles**. The change reaches them on their next click, and the audit log records who made it.

> Only someone who may change roles sees **Change roles**, and gives a role only when their own roles include everything it can do. An Owner holds no other role.

## Remove someone {#remove}

1. On the member’s row, open its actions and choose **Remove from the organization**.
2. Press **Remove**.

Their account stays theirs, and their work here keeps their name. Their access here ends on their next click, and to come back they need a new invitation. To leave yourself, use **Leave** on [Your account](/account#leave).

> Only someone who may remove members sees **Remove from the organization**, and removes only a member whose roles can do nothing beyond their own.

## Words you’ll meet {#words}

- **Owner**: everything, including costs, issuing invoices, settings and closing the organization.
- **Admin**: day-to-day work, such as stock, orders, production and drafting invoices; can invite and remove members, and change their roles.
- **Viewer**: looks at orders, stock and invoices, and changes nothing.
- **Made role**: a role the organization made for itself, such as Warehouse or Sales. It reads as named, in every language.
- **Permission**: one thing a role allows, such as **See prices on orders**.
- **Last active**: roughly when they last used the app.

## If something is refused {#refused}

- You confirm your own email address before you invite anyone, since invitations go out in your name. Use the link we sent you, or press **Resend confirmation**.
- An address that already belongs to a member is not invited. Change their roles instead.
- An address already invited is not invited twice. **Resend** the invitation instead.
- An organization makes at most 50 invitation links in 24 hours. The refusal says when you can invite again.
- A member keeps at least one role.
- A role that can do more than your own roles is not given, and a member whose roles can do more than yours is not changed or removed. An Owner can.
- The last Owner cannot lose the Owner role or be removed. Make another member an Owner first.
- Only an Owner can change or remove an Owner.

See also [People](/people) and [Roles](/settings/roles).
