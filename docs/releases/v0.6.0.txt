v0.6.0 — a product to work in

A look of its own, search and a Home page to find the work, files,
branding and product photos, the people one works with, licence
reminders, and a say in how long anyone stays signed in.

The look (ADR-055)
- A design of its own: a side rail (a drawer below 1200px), tabs on
  records with a summary beside them, one colour per status.
- Expiry shown as days left, in colour; money labelled before or
  including tax.
- Dark mode, larger controls on touch screens, WCAG 2.2 AA as the floor.
  Printed documents keep their own plain look.

Search (ADR-056)
- One lookup in the top bar (press /) across every kind of record the
  member may see, and a search box on every list.
- Chinese names found by their pinyin; close matches offered only when
  nothing closer is found.

Dates, sorting and export (ADR-057)
- Date ranges on invoices, credit notes, orders, stock movements,
  production runs and return authorizations, and a Credit notes list.
- Sorting where it was asked for: invoices by amount, orders by expected
  date, inventory by expiry.
- CSV export of what a list shows, recorded in the audit log.

Home (ADR-058)
- What needs attention, first: a card for each kind of waiting work,
  each opening the list it counts.
- Getting started for a new organization, and the records opened last.
- One kind of page for an unknown address, a missing record, no access,
  the server out of reach, or a crash.

Files (ADR-059)
- One store for every file, on Cloudflare R2, always served through the
  app's own address. Uploads are checked; photos are re-sized and their
  location data removed. 1 GB per organization. Copied nightly to the
  backup bucket.

The organization (ADR-060, ADR-061)
- Settings → Organization in tabs: Profile, Money, Documents, Stock,
  Branding and Security. The organization's name can now be changed;
  invoices already issued keep the old one.
- Each organization's logo (in the top bar and, if chosen, on printed
  documents), colour (six presets, or its own made readable), sidebar
  shade, and the days before expiry that turn a lot amber and red.

Product images (ADR-062, ADR-066)
- Up to eight images per product, added by dragging, pasting or
  choosing, put in order, with a cover.
- The cover shows in Products, Inventory and the lookup; a viewer zooms.

People (ADR-063)
- A photo of one's own, set only by that person; initials otherwise.
- A People page, a page per person, and when each was last active.

Notifications (ADR-064, #17)
- Licence expiry reminders 60, 30 and 7 days before, and on the day, to
  those who can update licences or release production.
- Every notification reads in the reader's own language.

Staying signed in (ADR-065)
- "Keep me signed in" at sign-in. Unticked, for a shared computer: signed
  out after two hours idle or twelve in all.
- An organization may sign people out after 15 minutes to 4 hours of
  inactivity (off by default), with a warning first.

Invoicing
- A refusal to issue for a missing address links to where it is set:
  Organization → Profile, or the customer's page.

Fixes
- Cancelling an order can no longer race another change to it (#26).
- Closing a run no longer tops up from stock held for a sale, and
  continues the lots the run was released with (#28).
- Leaving Raise an order with unsaved changes asks first (#54).
- Home loads within its performance budget.

Known limitations
- French and Chinese are still unreviewed drafts, marked beta in the
  language picker.
- An invoice that charges GST/HST can be issued without the
  organization's tax number. Set it on Organization → Profile before
  invoicing; a customer claiming input tax credits needs it on invoices
  of $100 or more.
- Licence reminders are in the app only, not by email, on fixed days.
- One gallery per product: no image of a variant's own, none on order
  lines or the shipping screen.
- No lock screen or switching user with a PIN, and no limit on a kept
  session beyond thirty days.
- No pro forma invoices; no deleting an account or leaving an
  organization.

Upgrade
- Migrations 0041 to 0054. Render runs them on deploy; elsewhere run
  `npm run migrate` in server.
- Once, against a database that already holds data: `npm run
  backfill:pinyin` in server, so existing Chinese names are found by
  pinyin.
- Files: `FILE_STORAGE=s3` with the `FILES_*` settings for the bucket
  (see .env.example); `local` keeps them on disk under server/storage/.
- Optional: `VITE_APP_NAME` names the product in the browser's tab.
