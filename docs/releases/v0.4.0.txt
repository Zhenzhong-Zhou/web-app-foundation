v0.4.0 — money

Everything a sale and a purchase are worth: invoices for what shipped,
authorized returns and partial credits, the cost of every lot and batch,
and price lists that propose the price of a new line.

Invoicing (ADR-046)
- An invoice bills exactly one shipment: draft, issued, voided.
- Issuing takes the next number in a gapless series per organization
  (INV-, CN-, RMA-), stores every amount, and copies the seller, bill-to
  and ship-to as they stood.
- Tax codes with one or more components; "Exempt" is a code with none.
  Nets round to the currency's minor units, tax once per component.
- A sale must be priced, in one currency, before it is confirmed. Samples
  are never invoiced.
- Void issues a full credit note and frees the shipment. A shipment with
  a standing invoice cannot be voided.
- "Mark shipped/received" is now "Close order" (#24).
- The organization's registered address and tax number, printed as the
  seller. Printable invoices and credit notes.

Returns and credit (ADR-047)
- A return authorization (RMA) is its own numbered document, with a
  resolution per line: credit, replace or none.
- A return may name an RMA and is held to it; one received without an
  RMA is still recorded and can be linked later, once.
- Credit notes credit part of an invoice, at a price that may be lowered
  but never raised, capped by value, by tax component and by what the RMA
  authorized.
- A replacement is a draft sale at zero, linked to its RMA.

Cost (ADR-048)
- Every stock movement is valued, in a ledger beside the quantities. A
  lot carries what it cost; stock without lots carries a running average.
- A purchase is valued at its order line's price, converted at the rate
  on file for the day it arrived. With no price or no rate it waits in a
  "needs a cost" list rather than being refused.
- A production run's material cost is posted to its batch at close; the
  run page shows the cost per unit and what it used.
- A cost set or corrected later revalues what is still on hand and
  records the difference for what has already gone.
- The organization's base currency, fixed once anything is valued in it,
  and an exchange-rate page, one rate per currency per day.
- Stock value page: what is on hand, lot by lot, and what is still waiting
  for a cost. Costs are visible to the Owner only by default.

Price lists (ADR-049)
- Sale and purchase lists, one currency each. A customer or supplier names
  its list; the organization sets a default for customers.
- An item added to an order without a price takes the list's, and keeps
  it: changing a list never changes an order already written. The order
  shows which list a price came from.
- "Use list price" applies the list again, on request.
- Samples never take a list price; an item whose list prices it in
  another currency than the sale is added unpriced, with the reason.

Screens
- Settings in the account menu: organization, tax codes, exchange rates,
  price lists, stock value.
- Cost panels on production runs and lot traces.

Known limitations
- Margin is not shown yet. Both an estimate on the order and the actual
  figure per invoice are derivable; the first real week decides which to
  build.
- Price lists have one price per item: no quantity breaks and no prices
  dated ahead.
- Cost is material only: no freight, duty, labour or co-packer charges. A
  corrected cost does not flow on into batches already made from it.
- Payments, and so gains and losses on exchange, are not recorded. A debt
  that will not be collected is a full credit without an RMA.
- The rate for a purchase is chosen by the UTC day of its arrival (#20).
- The weighted-average method should be confirmed with the accountant
  before these figures feed financial statements.
- A top-up at run close can still take stock held for a sale (#28).

Upgrade
- New migrations through 0035. Run `npm run migrate` in server, then
  `npm run seed` so existing Owners receive the new cost and price-list
  permissions.
- Set the base currency (Settings → Organization) before the first costed
  receipt. Stock already on hand opens at zero and appears under "needs a
  cost" until its cost is set.
