v0.5.0 — trust the numbers

Licences checked when a run is released, calendar days kept as days,
the app fast under load and backed up, and every screen in the reader's
language.

Licences at release (ADR-050)
- A run checks its product licence the day it is released: in force,
  expiring, expired or withdrawn, with the organization's policy for
  each (allow, ask for an override, or block).
- An override is recorded with its reason; a withdrawn licence always
  blocks.

Performance (ADR-051)
- Every list pages by keyset, not by offset.
- A Performance workflow seeds a large organization and checks query
  plans and response budgets (p95), on demand and weekly.

Calendar days (ADR-052, #20)
- Expected dates, expiry dates and document dates are calendar days,
  stored as dates, never shifted by a time zone.

Backups, phase 1 (ADR-053)
- A nightly logical backup to a separate bucket, kept 30 days and locked
  against deletion, and a monthly drill that restores one and checks it.

Languages (ADR-054)
- English, French (Canada) and Simplified Chinese, chosen per person.
- Documents print in the customer's language, or two side by side;
  product names in other languages, required where an organization says.
- Notifications and emails in the reader's language.

Fixes
- Duplicating an order no longer repeats what was received (#42).
- Dependencies updated.
