v0.7.0 — leaving, closing, and every document a PDF

Leaving an organization and deleting one's own account, closing an
organization after downloading everything in it, every document a PDF
drawn by the server, with three new ones, and orders with numbers of
their own.

Leaving and deleting an account (ADR-069)
- Account → Your data and your account: download your own data, leave
  the organization, or delete your account, confirmed with your
  password.
- The account goes 30 days later. Until then a banner on every page
  names the day, with Keep my account. A deletion that can no longer go
  ahead is cancelled, and the person is told why.
- The last Owner of an organization with other members makes someone
  else Owner first.
- Once deleted, the person is anonymized; the records they made and the
  audit log stay.

Closing an organization (ADR-070, #117)
- Download everything, at the end of Organization → Profile: one ZIP
  with a CSV per kind of record, the members, the files in use and a
  README.
- Close this organization: after downloading or declining to, with its
  name typed and the password. It closes 30 days later; any Owner can
  cancel until then.
- Every member sees a banner and is emailed when it is scheduled, a week
  before, if it is cancelled, and when it is done, in their own language.
- The only member can delete the organization and their account in one
  step.
- Once a deletion has begun erasing files, a cancel or an upload is
  refused rather than half done (#117).

Documents as PDF (ADR-071, #118)
- Every document is a PDF drawn by the server: one design, the same on
  every browser. Print opens it in the browser's own viewer; Download
  PDF saves it. The print pages are gone.
- A purchase order: Deliver by, the supplier's reference and where to
  deliver; an unpriced line prints blank with a request for its price,
  and the total counts priced lines, before tax.
- A pro forma invoice, from any priced sale not cancelled: taxed by the
  code chosen, as its invoice would be, marked Not a tax invoice, and
  recorded in the order's history with its total.
- A return authorization: the number to write on every box, what happens
  to each item, and a label to cut out while goods are expected.
- Every page names its document, its number and "Page 1 of 2". A
  purchase order and an RMA say who raised them: name, job title and
  work phone, never an email.
- Unit prices print to four places. Every character is drawn in a font
  that has it, so a line mixing Chinese with other letters prints whole.
- Paper per organization, on Organization → Documents: Letter, A4, or
  automatic from the country.

Orders and sites (ADR-071)
- Orders are numbered SO- and PO- when created, existing ones included.
  The number heads the order and names it everywhere: the list, the
  lookup, Home, invoices, RMAs and the lot trace.
- A site has an address of its own (Locations → Address). A purchase
  chooses Deliver to and an RMA Send back to, each one of the sites; the
  registered address prints when none is chosen or the site has none.

The export (ADR-071, ADR-057)
- Download everything adds a documents folder: every invoice and credit
  note as a PDF.
- Its README explains the columns, in the language the app shows.
- Every CSV writes a time as 2026-10-15 18:54:11, in UTC, which a
  spreadsheet opens as a date.

Invoicing (ADR-046)
- An invoice that charges tax is not issued until the organization has
  its tax number; the refusal links to Organization → Profile.

Under the hood (ADR-067, ADR-068)
- The server describes its API in OpenAPI, every response a declared
  shape that the tests check, and the client's types are generated from
  it.
- The server's tests on Vitest, and Nest 12.

Fixes
- An SVG upload could stop the server answering anyone; its check now
  takes linear time (#113).
- A skip link and a main landmark on every signed-in page (#121).
- The Account page shows and saves the account's own language (#119),
  and shows a download or a closing without a reload (#122).
- A refused password says what is wrong and marks its field, with Email
  me a reset link beside it (#120).
- A page that crashes can be left by the menu, Back or Go home, and
  offers Go back.
- The search shortcut no longer breaks when Chrome fills in a form.
- Dependencies updated.

Known limitations
- French and Chinese are still unreviewed drafts, marked beta in the
  language picker. Some of their refusals still name a language in
  English (#129).
- A packing slip's dates are in UTC until organizations have a time
  zone (#41): a shipment made in the evening in Vancouver shows the next
  day.
- A refused PDF opens as the server's bare message in a new tab (#128).
- An order's page shows its totals unrounded, where its purchase order
  rounds each line (#125).
- Documents are not emailed from the app; no payment details on
  invoices; no customs values, so a sample has no pro forma; an
  organization cannot yet set its own number prefixes.
- Licence reminders are in the app only, not by email, on fixed days.
- One gallery per product: no image of a variant's own, none on order
  lines or the shipping screen.
- No lock screen or switching user with a PIN, and no limit on a kept
  session beyond thirty days.

Upgrade
- Migrations 0055 to 0065 and the permission seed, which gives every
  existing Owner Download everything and Close this organization. Render
  runs both on deploy; elsewhere run `npm run migrate`, then `npm run
  seed`, in server.
- 0063 numbers every existing order, oldest first; 0065 sets Deliver to
  and Send back to on existing purchases and RMAs where the sites make
  it clear.
- `FILES_S3_ENDPOINT` must be the account's address alone, with the
  bucket in `FILES_BUCKET`; the server will not start otherwise (#92).
- The PDFs' fonts, about 20 MB, ship in server/assets/fonts; nothing is
  fetched.
